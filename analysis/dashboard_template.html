Priority table

Scoring formula. Shortfall = max(0, peer median − province score), in SD. Priority score = shortfall ÷ cost score. Cost score = mean of the group’s sub-index cost levels (low = 1, medium = 2, high = 3). Groups labelled strong priority candidate rank above all low reliability groups; within a tier the higher score ranks first. Horizon is shown for planning and is not scored. *Horizon and cost are team assumptions (pci_subindex_meta.csv), not measurements.
Label rule. The evidence control sets it. Both (default): strong priority candidate only if the 2025 association and the panel association have the same sign and the panel 95% interval excludes 0. 2025 only: the 2025 95% interval excludes 0. Panel only: the panel 95% interval excludes 0. In every mode the sign must be positive; otherwise the group is low reliability (or a flagged negative). Columns not used by the chosen rule are dimmed. The panel shows the % change in new firms per 1000 residents, k years later, per +1 SD in the group score, with province and year fixed effects and errors clustered by province.
